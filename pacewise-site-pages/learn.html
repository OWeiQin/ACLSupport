Understand the injury

What an ACL tear is, and why rehab takes months

The anterior cruciate ligament runs inside the knee and keeps the shin bone from sliding forward under the thigh bone. A tear usually happens during a sudden stop, twist or awkward landing.

The ligament heals slowly

A graft used in reconstruction goes through a long remodelling process. It is weakest in the early months, which is when feeling strong is most misleading.

Muscles protect the knee

The thigh muscles, hamstrings and hips share the load the ligament used to carry. Rehab is mostly about rebuilding that support.

Checks beat calendars

Timelines are typical ranges. What matters is meeting movement, strength and swelling checks, tested by your physio, before moving on. Return to sport is often around nine months or later.

The head recovers too

Fear of re-injury, frustration and impatience are common and affect how people train. They are part of the recovery plan, not a side issue.
